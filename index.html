On the road

A driver’s view, for a glance.

While you travel, the map sits behind your car and turns with the road. It shows your group, not turn-by-turn directions.

Your route, ahead of you

Only your own remaining route is drawn, so the road ahead stays clear.

Friends at the edge

Anyone off the map sits at the edge of the screen, on the side they really are, with how many minutes behind or ahead of you they’ll arrive.

One button for the whole picture

Tap the compass to zoom out to the whole group. Tap it again to come back.

Why not just share your location?

Because “where” isn’t “when”.

Every ETA, side by side

A live arrival time for each person and for the group. Location sharing shows where someone is; a maps app shares one driver’s ETA.

Delays announced

When someone’s running well behind, the group is told. Nobody has to ask.

iPhone and Android

One group, whatever phones they carry.

Privacy

Your location is borrowed, not kept.

Any Minute takes your location for the length of one trip, shows it to the people on it, and gives it back.

Read the privacy policy

Only while you travel

From Start the ride until your arrival countdown ends, 45 minutes at most. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Only your group

Only people the organiser let into the trip. An invite link on its own shows nothing.

Then deleted

One position per person, replaced as you move, so there’s no trail. It’s wiped when you stop sharing, and every route is deleted an hour after the trip is over.

Nothing else

No contacts, no phone number, no ads, no analytics. A home address you save never leaves your phone.
