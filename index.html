Common questions

My sign-in code never arrived

Check spam for a message from OMW. The code is six digits and lasts an hour. If nothing comes, wait a minute and tap Send a new code — if it still does not arrive, email us with the address you used.

How do I join a trip?

Tap the link the organiser sent you — it opens OMW and puts you on the trip. If you are already friends with them, the invitation appears on your Ride tab without a link at all. You will not see anyone's position until you answer whether you are coming; that is deliberate.

You need the app installed first — a link on its own will not install it.

Nobody can see me on the map

Three things have to be true: you answered Yes, you pressed Start, and location permission is set to Always. Always is what keeps your ETA accurate while you are following a navigation app; without it your dot goes quiet the moment you leave OMW. You can change it in your phone's Settings under OMW.

How do I stop sharing?

Open the trip's settings from the map and tap Stop sharing my location. It works instantly, and you stay on the trip. Sharing also stops by itself when you arrive.

Can I be on the trip without sharing my location?

Yes — answer Just watching (or Just hosting if you organised it). You see everyone on the map and send nothing.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: the You tab, then Delete my account at the bottom. It removes your email, name, username, every position, the trips you host and your seat on everyone else's. It cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for what is kept and for how long.

Why does it want background location?

Because you will be driving with a map app open in front of OMW. Without it your position stops updating the moment you switch away, and the group sees you go quiet on the road — which is the exact problem the app exists to remove. It only ever runs while a trip you joined is running.

Reporting a problem

The You tab has Report a problem, which includes your app version and phone model automatically. That is the fastest route for a bug. For anything about your account or your data, email us instead.
