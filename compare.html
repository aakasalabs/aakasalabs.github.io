Where isn’t when.

How Any Minute differs from the apps you already have. As of November 2026.

Location sharing answers “where are they?”. A maps app’s ETA share answers “when will they get here?” for one driver. Any Minute answers it for everyone heading to the same place, on one screen.

When another app is the better choice

  • One person driving to you. Their maps app can send you their arrival time, and you don’t need anything new to see it.
  • Knowing where family is all day. That is what location sharing apps are for. Any Minute shares only while you travel to a trip, and stops by itself.

When Any Minute fits best

When several of you are heading to one place from different directions: dinner, a family visit, an airport pickup, game day. Everyone can see who is close and who is running late, so nobody has to ask.

Your location is shared only with the people on that trip, and every position is deleted an hour after the trip is over. The privacy policy has the details.
