OMW — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026 · Aakasa Labs

OMW shows a group travelling to one place where each other are, so nobody has to ask. This policy says exactly what that costs in data. It is short because the app collects little.

Location, specifically

  • It is sent only while a trip is running and only after you say you are coming. It stops on its own when you arrive.
  • It continues while the app is in the background, because you will be following a navigation app rather than this one. iOS shows the location indicator and Android shows a notification the whole time.
  • Your position is replaced as you move, not added to. We keep one coordinate per person per trip, so there is no history to hand over, sell or lose.
  • Every coordinate and route is deleted an hour after the last person arrives. What remains is the trip itself — its name, where it was going, who was on it and when they got there.
  • Nothing is sent when no trip is running.

Who can see it

Only people on the same trip who have answered the invitation. Opening an invite link shows you nothing until you reply. Joining, answering, arriving and leaving are recorded and shown to everyone on that trip — names and times only, never positions — so nobody can watch a group and then erase that they were there.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosts the database and sends your sign-in code.
  • Google Maps, Places and Routes — the map, place search and drive times.
  • Apple and Google push services — delivering notifications to your device.

They process this data to run the app and nothing else. We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. The iOS privacy manifest declares OMW as not tracking.

What we never collect

No contacts or address book, no phone number, no photos, no browsing history, no advertising identifier. Your email is never searchable and never shown to other users. A home address you save stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Deleting everything

In the app: You → Account → Delete my account. It removes your email, name, username, every position, the trips you host and your seat on everyone else's — on the server and on the device. It cannot be undone. You can also stop sharing at any point during a trip without leaving it.

Children

OMW is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the app's Privacy screen is updated in the same release.
