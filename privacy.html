Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026 · Aakasa Labs

Any Minute shows a group heading to one place where each of them is, so nobody has to ask. This policy says exactly what that costs in data. It is short because the app collects little.

What we collect, and why

Location, specifically

  • It is sent only while you are on a trip, from the moment you start it. When you arrive it keeps going for 15 minutes so the people on your trip can find you, and then stops on its own. If others arrive after you it can run longer, but never more than 45 minutes after your own arrival. You can end it at any point with Stop.
  • It continues while the app is in the background, because you will be following a navigation app rather than this one. iPhone shows the location indicator and Android shows a notification the whole time.
  • Your position is replaced as you move, not added to. We keep one position per person per trip, so there is no history to hand over, sell or lose.
  • Your live position is wiped as soon as you stop sharing. Every position and route is deleted an hour after the trip is over: an hour after the last traveller is heard from, or after the planned arrival time if that is later. After that we hold no position for anyone on the trip: not the last one, not the route, not where they set off. What remains is the trip itself: its name, where it was going, who was on it and when they got there.
  • Nothing is sent when you are not on a trip.

Who can see it

Only people the organiser has let in to the same trip. Opening an invite link shows you nothing until you are let in. Joining, arriving and leaving are recorded and shown to everyone on that trip, as names and times only and never positions, so nobody can watch a group and then erase that they were there.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sends your sign-in code.
  • Google Maps, Places and Routes provide the map, place search and drive times.
  • Apple and Google push services deliver notifications to your device.

They process this data to run the app and nothing else. We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking code in the app. To know where Any Minute is used, we keep the country your account was last used from, as two letters. The internet address it comes from is not stored, and the country is deleted with your account. The iPhone privacy manifest declares Any Minute as not tracking.

What we never collect

No contacts or address book, no phone number, no photos, no browsing history, no advertising identifier. Your email is never searchable and never shown to other users. A home address you save stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Deleting everything

In the app, go to You → Delete my account. It removes your email, name, username, every position, the trips you host and your place on everyone else’s, on the server and on the device. It cannot be undone. You can also stop sharing at any point during a trip without leaving it. Without the app, see how to ask us to delete it.

Children

Any Minute is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the app’s Privacy screen is updated in the same release.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to see or delete your data. We answer within a few days.
