Tester guide

What to try, what we want to know, and how to tell us

Thank you for trying Any Minute. It isn’t finished, and the point of you being here is to find out where it falls over.

It needs two people. One person alone sees an empty map and learns nothing. Arrange to go somewhere with someone else who has it. Even a ten-minute drive to a supermarket is enough.

Getting it

  1. iPhone: install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the public beta link and install Any Minute from TestFlight.
  2. Android: there’s no Play Store listing yet. We send you the installer directly.

Signing in

Enter your email and you get a six-digit code; there is no password. The first one often lands in spam, so mark it as not spam and later ones won’t. Then set your name.

What to try

  1. Read the First things first page. Tell us if anything on it is unclear or feels like it asks for too much.
  2. One of you plans a trip and invites the other.
  3. When you set off, press Start the trip.
  4. Actually travel, with the app in your pocket and your usual maps app on screen, while the other person watches.
  5. Stop somewhere for a few minutes on the way. Then carry on.

What we most want to know

  • Did anyone appear stuck, stale or out of contact while their phone was clearly fine? This matters most.
  • Was an arrival time ever wrong enough that you would have phoned someone anyway?
  • After you arrived, could you find each other, and did sharing stop by itself when the countdown ran out?
  • Was there any moment you didn’t know what the app was telling you?

Known, and not worth reporting

  • An invite link doesn’t open the app for someone who hasn’t installed it yet.
  • If iPhone closes Any Minute in the background, sharing stops until you reopen it.

How to report something

For behaviour, meaning anything about position, timing or a wrong state, use You → Report a problem in the app. It sends your words along with what your phone actually did: which build, which positions it sent and when, and any crash from the previous session. You see all of it before it goes. That trail is usually what makes a report fixable.

For anything you can see, like a layout problem or wrong wording: on iPhone, take a screenshot, tap Share and choose Send Beta Feedback. On Android, use the button below and attach the screenshot.

Either way, what you were doing at the time is worth more than what you thought was wrong.

What we can see

While a trip is running, your position goes to the people on that trip and nobody else. It is never sold and never used for advertising, and every position is erased an hour after the trip is over. The privacy policy has the detail.

Something you’d rather say directly? We read every message.
