Testing OMW

Thank you for trying this. OMW shows everyone travelling to one place where the others are, so nobody has to send “where are you?”. It is not finished, and the point of you being here is to find out where it falls over.

It needs two people. One person alone sees an empty map and learns nothing. Before you judge it, arrange to go somewhere with someone else who also has it — even a ten-minute drive to a supermarket is enough.

Getting it

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invitation you were sent and accept it.
  3. Install OMW from TestFlight and open it.

iPhone only for now. There is no Android build yet.

Signing in

Enter your email and you get a six-digit code — there is no password. The first one often lands in spam; mark it as not spam so later ones do not. Then set your first and last name.

What to try

  1. Read the First things first page. Tell us if anything on it is unclear or feels like it is asking for too much.
  2. One of you starts a trip and shares the code. The other joins with it.
  3. Say Yes when asked whether you are coming, then press Start.
  4. Actually travel, with the app in your pocket and your usual maps app on screen, while the other person watches.
  5. Stop somewhere for a few minutes on the way. Then carry on.

What we most want to know

  • Did anyone appear stuck, stale or “contact lost” while their phone was clearly fine? This is the most important thing on the list.
  • Was an arrival time ever wrong enough that you would have phoned someone anyway?
  • Did sharing stop by itself when you arrived?
  • Was there any moment you did not know what the app was telling you?

Known, and not worth reporting

  • Android does not exist yet.
  • Notifications other than “time to leave” are not delivered.
  • A shared link does not open the app for someone who has not installed it — use the typed code.
  • If iOS closes OMW in the background, sharing stops until you reopen it.

How to report something

For behaviour — anything about position, timing or a wrong state — use Settings → Feedback in the app. It sends your words along with what your device actually did: which build, which positions it sent and when, and any crash from the previous session. You see all of it before it goes. That trail is usually what makes a report fixable, so this route is much more useful than a message.

For anything you can see — a layout problem, wrong wording, something ugly — take a screenshot, tap Share, and choose Send Beta Feedback. That reaches us with your device and build attached.

Either way, what you were doing at the time is worth more than what you thought was wrong.

What we can see

While a ride is running, your position goes to the people on that ride and nobody else. It is never sold and never used for advertising. Every coordinate is erased an hour after the last person arrives — what stays is who came and when. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Sharing only happens while a ride you started is running. There is no ride running by default, and there is nothing to turn off between them.
